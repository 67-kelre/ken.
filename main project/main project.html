About scout Scout

scouts laws

  • Scout Law teaches us to be honest and trustworthy.
  • Scouts should protect the environment and keep their surroundings clean.
  • Scouts should be cheerful and stay positive during difficult times.
  • Scouts must respect their parents, teachers, leaders, and elders.
  • A good scout is kind, friendly, and polite to everyone.
  • Scouts must be loyal to their country, family, and friends.
  • Scouts should work together and support their teammates.
  • Scouts should be disciplined and responsible for their actions.
  • Scouts must respect public property and keep their community safe.
  • Scouts should use their time wisely and do good things for others.
  • Scouts must always speak the truth and keep their promises.

Important of Scout

  • Bhutan Scouts help students develop good character and positive attitudes.
  • They teach young people to become responsible citizens who contribute to the country's development.
  • Scouts learn the importance of Gross National Happiness, which emphasizes well-being and happiness.
  • They encourage students to preserve Bhutanese culture, traditions, and values.
  • Scouts learn to respect the environment and protect forests, rivers, and wildlife.
  • They develop leadership skills by taking part in scout activities and training programmes.
  • Scouts learn to work together, share ideas, and respect one another.
  • They participate in social work and community service to make their communities better.
  • Scouting helps students become physically active, mentally strong, and emotionally balanced.
  • Bhutan Scouts prepare young people to become future leaders who serve the nation with compassion and integrity.
Bhutan Scouts is a national youth organization dedicated to developing young people through leadership, teamwork, discipline, service, and outdoor activities. It provides opportunities for children and youth to participate in activities such as camping, community service, environmental conservation, adventure, and leadership training. Through these experiences, Bhutan Scouts encourages values such as responsibility, respect, kindness, loyalty, and a strong spirit of service to others and the community. Its aim is to help young people become confident, capable, responsible, and active citizens who can contribute positively to the development of Bhutan.
