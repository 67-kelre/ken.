About Scout

Scouts is a youth organization that helps young people develop good character, discipline, confidence, leadership, and teamwork. Scouts learn useful skills such as camping, hiking, first aid, cooking, and navigation. They also take part in community service, environmental activities, games, and outdoor adventures. Scouting teaches young people to be responsible, helpful, respectful, and ready to serve others. It is a great way to learn new skills, make friends, and become a better and more responsible person.

  • Scouts are young people who take part in the Scouting movement, which helps them learn useful skills and become responsible members of society. Scouts participate in activities such as camping, hiking, games, and outdoor adventures. These activities help them become confident, active, and independent.
  • Scouting also teaches important values such as discipline, teamwork, leadership, honesty, and kindness. Scouts learn practical skills like first aid, cooking, tying knots, and protecting the environment. They work together and help one another during different activities..
  • Bhutan Scouts is a youth organization in Bhutan that helps young people develop good character, leadership skills, discipline, and a sense of responsibility. It teaches Scouts to be helpful, honest, respectful, and kind to others. Bhutan Scouts encourages young people to serve their communities, protect the environment, respect Bhutanese culture and traditions, and help people in need. Scouts take part in activities such as camping, hiking, teamwork, community service, and environmental conservation. Through these activities, they learn important life skills, build confidence, and become responsible citizens. Bhutan Scouts plays an important role in promoting the values of GrossNational Happiness and helping young people contribute to the development of Bhutan
